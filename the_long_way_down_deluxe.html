The ocean is changing

The same water that shields us from our own excess is now absorbing the cost.

Heat

The ocean has absorbed about 90% of the extra heat trapped by greenhouse gases, and marine heatwaves are becoming longer and more frequent.

Acidity

It takes up roughly a quarter to a third of our CO₂ emissions. That lowers seawater pH, which makes it harder for corals, oysters and plankton to build shells.

Plastic

Around 8 million tonnes of plastic enter the ocean each year. It has been found from surface gyres to the deepest trenches.

Overfishing

More than a third of assessed fish stocks are fished beyond sustainable levels. Well-managed fisheries and protected areas let stocks recover.

Figures are rounded and vary slightly between sources such as NOAA and the International Hydrographic Organization. Deepest point: about 10,935 m (Challenger Deep, Mariana Trench).
